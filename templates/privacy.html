Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, and the financial transactions you log within the application. We may also collect basic device information to ensure a stable and secure user experience.

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking services you've signed up for. This includes calculating spending patterns, generating summaries, and managing your user account. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We implement strict access controls to ensure that your financial information is protected from unauthorized access and is only accessible to you and the system processes required to run the app.

We may use third-party infrastructure providers for hosting and database services. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and are only permitted to process data as necessary to provide their services to us.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of your data, please contact our support team through the application's contact channels.
